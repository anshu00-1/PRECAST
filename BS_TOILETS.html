Precast Toilets

Concrete surface is painted with anti-carbonization paints making it long lasting and durable. The toilets can be either western or Indian style.

STANDARD SPECIFICATIONS
1 Shell Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
2 Wall paints Poly urethane based paints for internal and external surfaces
3 Wall Dado Poly urethane coatings, which can be washed with standard cleaning liquids/ detergents
4 Floor Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
5 Door Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
6 Louvers Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
7 Electrical Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
8 Closet Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
9 Tap Monolithic Precast RC Concrete of M50 Grade
